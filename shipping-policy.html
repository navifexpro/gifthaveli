Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver all over Pakistan. We do not deliver outside Pakistan, but you can order from anywhere in the world for someone in Pakistan.

Delivery charges

Delivery is Rs 250 per order. Free on orders above Rs 5,000. The delivery charge is shown in your basket before you place the order.

Delivery date

Every gift is made to order. You choose a delivery date when you order. We confirm the date with you on WhatsApp after we check your order. If we cannot make it on that date, we will tell you before we start.

Tracking

You get an Order ID when you place your order. Use it on the Track Order page to see when your gift is confirmed, being made, shipped and delivered.

Wrong address

Please check the receiver's name, phone number and address. If the address is wrong or the receiver cannot be reached, delivery may be late and extra delivery charges may apply.
