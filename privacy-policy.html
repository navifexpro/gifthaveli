Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What we collect

When you order, we collect your name, WhatsApp number and country, the receiver's name, phone number and address, the delivery date, your gift message, and any photo or text you add to a customized gift.

How we use it

We use this information only to make, deliver and support your order, and to talk to you about it on WhatsApp. We do not sell your information.

Your photos

Photos you upload are saved privately with your order so we can make your gift. We do not post your photos or your gift on our social media without your permission.

Who we share it with

We share the receiver's name, phone number and address only with the delivery company, so they can deliver the gift.

Keeping it safe

Order details are kept in our private order records. Only the Gift Haveli team can see them.

Your basket

Your basket is saved in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. We do not use advertising cookies on this website.
