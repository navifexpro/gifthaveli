Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Customized gifts

Customized gifts are made only for you, with your name, photo or message. Because of this, we cannot take them back or refund them if you change your mind.

Damaged or wrong item

If your gift arrives damaged, or it is not what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp 0313-0111349 as soon as you receive it, with photos of the gift and the packaging. We will check it and remake the item or give you a refund for that item.

Mistakes in your details

We print exactly the name, text and photo you send. Please check spelling and photo quality before you order. We cannot remake a gift for free if the mistake was in the details sent to us.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel before we start making your gift. Once we have started, we cannot cancel a customized order.

How refunds are paid

If a refund is approved, we send it back the same way you paid, or by bank transfer. We will confirm the details with you on WhatsApp.
